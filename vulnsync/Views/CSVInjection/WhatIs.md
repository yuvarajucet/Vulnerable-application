## What is CSV Injection?
`CSV Injection`, also known as Formula Injection, occurs when an attacker is able to inject malicious formulas into a CSV file that is opened in spreadsheet applications like Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets. This can lead to unauthorized actions, data manipulation, or exposure of sensitive information when the file is opened and the formulas are executed.
