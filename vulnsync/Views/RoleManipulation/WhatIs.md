## What is Role Manipulation?
**Role manipulation** is a security vulnerability where an attacker alters their assigned user role within an application, such as changing their role from 'user' to 'admin.' This typically occurs when role data is insecurely handled on the client-side, making it vulnerable to tampering. If successful, it can grant unauthorized privileges and access. Proper validation and authorization checks are essential to prevent this risk.
