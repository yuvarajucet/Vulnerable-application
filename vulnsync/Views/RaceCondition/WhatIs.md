## What is a Race Condition Vulnerability?

A `race condition vulnerability` happens when a system's behavior depends on the timing or order of events. Attackers exploit this to gain unauthorized access, corrupt data, or disrupt operations. It occurs when multiple processes or threads try to access and modify shared resources (like files or memory) at the same time without proper coordination.
