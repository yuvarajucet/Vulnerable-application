## External Login Account Takeover
`External Login Account Takeover` occurs when attackers exploit vulnerabilities in `third-party
login systems`, such as `OAuth or OpenID Connect`, to gain `unauthorized access` to user `accounts`. 
By manipulating login flows, intercepting authentication tokens, or using stolen credentials,
attackers bypass traditional security measures and gain control over accounts on targeted platforms.
Common techniques include `credential stuffing`, `phishing`, and `session hijacking`, where attackers reuse or intercept tokens for unauthorized access.
