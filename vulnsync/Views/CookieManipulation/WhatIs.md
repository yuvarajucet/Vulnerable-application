## What is Cookie Manipulation?
**Cookie manipulation** refers to the process of accessing, modifying, or tampering with cookies stored in a user's browser to achieve a desired behavior. It can involve setting, updating, or deleting cookies to manage user sessions, preferences, or authentication. While it is commonly used for legitimate purposes like session management, malicious manipulation can lead to security vulnerabilities, such as session hijacking or unauthorized access. Proper validation and security measures (e.g., encryption, secure flags) are essential to prevent misuse.
